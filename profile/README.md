## AJ Series & Aaahhh Fonts Vibe `v3.1.0`

**5 handcrafted families · 27 styles · 108 binaries · 25 languages · 14 world scripts · 4,513 Unicode codepoints**

| Family | Level | Styles |
|---|---|---|
| **AJ Sahasvi** | Level 1 Headers (`H1`) · Royal Small-Caps | 3 |
| **AJ Shresti** | Level 2 Sub-Headings, Active OS UI & Subtitles | 6 |
| **AJ Talin** | Level 3 Handcrafted Calligraphic Script (`0°` + `11°`) | 6 |
| **AJ Anushya** | Long-Form Body Reading & Low-DPI OCR Clarity | 6 |
| **Aaahhh Fonts Vibe** | *Sound of Font* — 25-Language Smart Phonetic Suite | 6 |

**Sound of Font:** OpenType phonetic shaping in `Aaahhh Fonts Vibe` through `:lang:word` triggers and stylistic sets `ss06–ss18`, paired with smart spell/grammar correction and document-context inline helptext. Numbers and currency stay strictly untouched.

- **One-Click OS Installers:** https://fonts.aivibe.cloud/install
- **Sound of Font Studio:** https://fonts.aivibe.cloud/sound-of-font
- **25-Language Specimen Hub:** https://fonts.aivibe.cloud/lang
